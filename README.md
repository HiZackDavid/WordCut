# 📖 Description

WordCut is a challenging mobile word puzzle game where players must strategically deconstruct words to win. Starting with a word of at least 5 letters, your goal is to create shorter valid words by removing 1, 2, or 3 letters at a time until you reach a final word of 1 to 3 letters
